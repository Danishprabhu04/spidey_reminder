Installing

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download

    Hit the download button on an app. The site picks the right file for your computer.

  2. 2

    Open the file

    Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” — click More info → Run anyway. My apps aren't code-signed yet.

  3. 3

    Enjoy

    Click Install, then Finish. To remove an app later: Settings → Apps.
