What he does

Small spider. Sixteen superpowers.

🎭 Personality

  • Elastic web physics — drag him anywhere, let go and he springs back, spins and wobbles.
  • Moods & expressions — happy, proud, worried about overdue work, sleepy at night, dizzy after a fling.
  • Eyes follow your cursor — and he flinches if you sneak up on him.
  • Celebrations — backflips and web-confetti every time you finish something.

📝 Missions

  • Type like a human — “call mom tomorrow 5pm !high” sets the date, time and priority for you.
  • Quick add anywhere — double-tap Spidey or press a global shortcut.
  • Focus timer — Pomodoro sessions with a countdown ring on his chest.
  • Streaks & stats — daily streak, week chart and focus minutes.

⏰ Smart reminders

  • Due-time alerts — he drops down the screen with a desktop notification.
  • Knows when you're away — waits while you're idle, locked or in a fullscreen game.
  • Morning & end-of-day brief — what's on today, and what you got done.
  • Snooze — 10 minutes, an hour or tomorrow morning.

🖥️ Your desktop

  • Web dashboard — manage everything from your browser, live.
  • Multi-monitor — swing him onto any screen; he remembers where he lives.
  • Do not disturb — hide him for an hour or until tomorrow.
  • Sounds & settings — thwips, boings and cheers, all tweakable.

Install

Pick your computer.

Download for Windows

  1. Open SpideySense-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
  3. Click Install, then Finish. Spidey appears at the top of your screen.

Prefer a command?

Paste into PowerShell:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Danishprabhu04/spidey_reminder/main/hub/products/spidey-sense/install.ps1 | iex

Uninstall any time from Settings → Apps → Spidey Sense.

Paste into a terminal — installs for you only, no sudo:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Danishprabhu04/spidey_reminder/main/hub/products/spidey-sense/install.sh | bash

Adds Spidey to your app menu and starts him at login. Tested on Ubuntu 22.04 & 24.04 (GNOME, Wayland and X11).

Or the AppImage

Download AppImage

Right-click → Properties → Allow executing, then double-click.

To uninstall, run the command above with bash -s -- --uninstall at the end.

Download for macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Spidey Sense into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app → Open (it isn't notarized yet).

Prefer a command?

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Danishprabhu04/spidey_reminder/main/hub/products/spidey-sense/install.sh | bash

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free, and no account. Spidey keeps everything in a small database on your own computer.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

New apps without a paid code-signing certificate get a SmartScreen warning. Click More info → Run anyway. The installer only writes to your user folder and never asks for admin rights.

Will he get in my way?

Clicks pass straight through everywhere except Spidey himself. He waits while you're away or in a fullscreen app, and Do not disturb hides him completely. Left-click his tray icon to hide or show him.

How do I add a mission quickly?

Double-tap Spidey, or press Win+Alt+M on Windows / Super+Shift+M on Linux. Write naturally: “pay rent friday 9am !high”.

How do I uninstall?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Spidey Sense → Uninstall. Linux/macOS: run the install command with --uninstall (add --purge to delete your missions too).
